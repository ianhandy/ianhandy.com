Polymerge Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Polymerge does not collect personal data. It has no ads, analytics, tracking, accounts, or third-party advertising SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Polymerge saves game progress, high scores, preferences, accessibility choices, and other gameplay state locally on your device. Ian Handy does not receive or have access to this information.

Device motion

If shake-to-nudge is enabled, Polymerge reads device-motion measurements to move shapes during play. Motion data is processed on your device while the feature is active. It is not stored, transmitted, or used for tracking. You can disable the feature in the app's settings.

Network access and third parties

Polymerge does not send gameplay or personal information to Ian Handy. It does not include accounts, advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Apple may process information independently when you download, update, back up, or review the app. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Polymerge does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes

If Polymerge's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to ian@ianhandy.com.
