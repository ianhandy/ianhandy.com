Polymerge Support

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Polymerge is a physics merge game for iPhone, built by Ian Handy.

For help or bug reports, email ian@ianhandy.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Polymerge version, and what happened.

Common questions

How do I turn off shake-to-nudge?

Open Polymerge's settings and turn off device motion. The app stops device-motion updates when the feature is disabled.

How do I reset my progress?

Use the reset option in the app's settings. Resetting removes locally stored progress and cannot be undone.

The app is not behaving correctly

Close and reopen Polymerge, then make sure iOS and the app are up to date. If the problem continues, email the details above and include a screenshot or screen recording when possible.
