stowi Support

stowi is a private, local-first home inventory for iPhone: scan a room, place things in it, and ask Siri where they are later.

Need help with something not covered here?

Email ian@ianhandy.com — describe what happened and, if it's a bug, what you expected instead.

Getting started

stowi seeds a sample room the first time you open it, so there's something to look at right away. From the room tab, the + button in the composer at the bottom lets you add an item, add a container, or open the camera — those three are always available, wherever you're standing in the app. The ⋯ button in the top right opens everything else: scanning a room, editing the map, placing a container, adding a room, and units.

Scanning a room

Open the ⋯ menu and choose Scan Floor Plan. On a device with LiDAR (iPhone Pro models), this uses Apple's RoomPlan to read the room's walls, doorways, and floor area. A new scan replaces the room's shape — but never touches the containers, items, or landmarks you've already placed in it.

Using Siri

stowi supports two things through Siri, once you've opened the app at least once:

  • Tell it where something is — "Hey Siri, tell stowi the drill is in the gray box by the workbench."
  • Ask where something is — "Hey Siri, ask stowi where the drill is."

stowi figures out which room you mean by matching a landmark or container name in what you say. If it can't tell — or if the same name exists in more than one room — it says so instead of guessing, and asks you to be more specific or open the app.

Siri isn't responding

Open stowi once after installing or updating it — Siri needs the app to have launched at least once to register its shortcuts. If it still doesn't respond, check Settings → Siri & Search → stowi to confirm Siri access is on.

Free vs. Full Version

The free version is the complete app, limited to one room and up to three containers. The Full Version is a one-time $4.99 purchase that removes those two limits — nothing you've already added is ever hidden, locked, or made read-only if you don't buy it. Restore a previous purchase from the same prompt with Restore Purchases.

Your data

Open the ⋯ menu (you may need to scroll it) for two controls: Export Inventory, which saves everything in your home as a JSON file you can keep or share, and Delete All Data, which permanently clears every room, container, item, and history entry. See the privacy policy for exactly what stowi does and doesn't do with your information.

Permissions

stowi asks for camera, photo library, microphone, and speech-recognition access as you use the features that need them — never all at once. If you said no by mistake, or want to change your mind, you can turn any of them back on in Settings → stowi.
