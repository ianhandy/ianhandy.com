// stowi / Privacy Policy

Your home stays yours.

Last updated August 18, 2026

stowi is a home-inventory app for iPhone. This policy explains what stowi does and doesn't do with your information, in plain terms. The short version: stowi keeps your inventory on your phone, doesn't create an account for you, doesn't run analytics or advertising trackers, and doesn't sell or share your data with anyone. What follows is the complete, specific version.

Where your data lives

Everything you add to stowi — rooms, room layouts, containers, items, photos, notes, and your organizing history — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework. None of it is uploaded to a server stowi operates, and stowi does not use iCloud sync. If you delete the app, that data is gone with it — which is also why the app has its own built-in Export Inventory feature: it writes your whole inventory to a JSON file you can save or share yourself, whenever you want a copy.

Camera and photo library

stowi asks for camera access so you can photograph the things you're putting away, and photo library access so you can choose an existing picture instead. Photos are compressed and stored in the local database described above. They are not uploaded anywhere as part of normal use.

Microphone and speech recognition

stowi asks for microphone and speech-recognition access so you can describe what you're organizing out loud instead of typing. This transcription is configured to run entirely on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognizer — your voice is never sent to Apple's servers or anyone else's for this feature.

On-device suggestions

When you photograph an item, stowi uses Apple's on-device Vision framework, and on compatible devices Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework, to suggest a name and category. This analysis happens on your iPhone. Nothing about the photo or the suggestion is sent off the device by this feature.

Siri and Shortcuts

stowi supports Siri for hands-free capture and lookup — for example, "Hey Siri, tell stowi the drill is in the gray box." When you use Siri, your spoken request is handled by Apple's Siri and Shortcuts systems according to Apple's own privacy policy, and stowi only receives the resulting text once Siri has processed it.

Optional photo-recognition service

stowi's code includes support for an optional, separately-hosted photo-recognition service that could send a compressed item photo to a third-party AI provider to help suggest a name. This feature is not enabled in this release. stowi never sends a photo anywhere by default, and won't unless a future version turns this on explicitly — in which case this policy will be updated first to say so plainly.

Purchases

stowi's optional Full Version purchase is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. stowi never sees or stores your payment information — Apple does, under its own privacy policy.

What stowi doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs
  • No location tracking
  • No data sold or shared with third parties for marketing

Deleting your data

stowi has a built-in Delete All Data option that permanently clears every room, container, item, and history entry on your device. Uninstalling the app does the same thing, since nothing is stored anywhere else.

Children's privacy

stowi is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age — it doesn't collect information from anyone at all, in the sense of data leaving your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters — most importantly, if any optional network feature is ever turned on by default — the date at the top of this page will change, and the change will be called out in the app's release notes.
