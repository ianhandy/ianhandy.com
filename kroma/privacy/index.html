// Kromatika / Privacy Policy

No account. No tracking.

Last updated August 18, 2026

Kromatika is a color puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what it does and doesn't do with your information, in plain terms. The short version: there's no account, no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no tracking of any kind. Your progress lives on your phone. A few features do talk to a server, and each one is described specifically below.

What stays on your device

Your level, hearts, campaign progress, stats, daily-streak calendar, accessibility settings, saved gallery puzzles and collections, and your block and report lists are all stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

Three of those — your progress, aggregate stats, and daily-completion calendar — are also written to your own private iCloud key-value storage, so they follow you to another device signed in to the same Apple Account. That data goes to Apple's iCloud under your account, not to a server the developer can read.

The identifier Kromatika uses

For the online features below, the app generates a random ID the first time it needs one and stores it in your device's Keychain. It is not your Apple Account, not your name, not your device's advertising identifier, and not linked to your identity. Its only jobs are to stop the same device from voting twice, and to let you remove your own streak entry from the public board later. It is stored so that it never leaves the device it was created on, except as part of the requests described here.

Features that send something

Today's puzzle

The app asks the Kromatika server for the day's puzzle by date. This request sends no information about you. If it fails, the app builds the day's puzzle locally instead and remains fully playable offline.

Sharing a puzzle

When you share a puzzle you made, the puzzle itself is uploaded so a short link can be created for it. That upload contains the puzzle's colors and layout — not your name, unless you typed one into the puzzle.

Community puzzles

Submitting a puzzle to the community pool is something you choose to do per puzzle. It uploads the puzzle, the random ID above, and whatever name you typed in the name field, which is then shown publicly to other players alongside your puzzle. Don't put anything there you don't want visible. Every submission is reviewed before anyone else sees it. Voting on a puzzle sends your vote and the random ID.

Reporting a puzzle sends the puzzle's identifier, the reason you picked, and the random ID. Blocking and hiding are entirely local — nothing about them is uploaded, and they take effect on your device immediately.

Liked puzzles

Rating a puzzle with the thumbs up or down at the end of a level sends that rating and the puzzle's layout to the server, so well-liked puzzles can be offered to other players. It does not include your name.

The streak leaderboard — off by default

Appearing on the public longest-streak board is opt-in. Until you turn it on, nothing about your streak leaves your phone. If you do turn it on, the app sends the random ID, your longest streak, and the date you completed through — no name and no email. Turning it back off deletes your entry from the board.

Feedback

If you send feedback from inside the app, it goes to a Google Form, and is therefore also handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. It includes your ratings, anything you type in the notes box, and technical details about the puzzle you were on — the app shows you that attached block before you send, so you can see exactly what's going. It doesn't include your name, email, or the random ID.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, scores and achievements are submitted to Apple's Game Center, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy. The app doesn't receive or store your Game Center identity.

Photos

Kromatika asks for permission to add photos only when you tap save on a finished puzzle, so it can put that image in your library. It cannot read your photo library, and it never uploads images anywhere.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily streak reminder, the notification is scheduled locally on your device. There is no push server and no notification token is collected.

Purchases

The Full Version is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Kromatika never sees or stores your payment information — Apple does, under its own privacy policy.

What Kromatika doesn't do

  • No account, sign-in, email address, or password
  • No advertising, ad SDKs, or advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party SDKs at all
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No location, contacts, microphone, or camera access
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone for marketing

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To remove your entry from the public streak board, turn streak sharing off in the app before deleting it — or email the address below and it can be removed for you. Puzzles you submitted to the community pool can also be removed on request.

Children's privacy

Kromatika is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. It never asks for a name, email, age, or any other personal detail. The one field that becomes public is the optional name you may type when submitting a puzzle to the community pool, which is reviewed before it appears.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top of this page will change and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.
