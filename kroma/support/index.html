Kromatika Support

Kromatika is a color-ordering puzzle for iPhone: every row and column is a gradient, and your job is to put each color back where it belongs.

Need help, or want to report something?

Email help@ianhandy.com — describe what happened and, if it's a bug, what you expected instead. Reports about community puzzles or player names are read at this address too.

How the puzzle works

Each puzzle is made of one or more gradients — straight runs of cells where the color steps evenly from one end to the other. Some cells are already filled in as clues. The colors you need for the empty ones sit in the bank at the bottom; drag one onto a cell, or tap the color and then tap the cell. Where two gradients cross, the shared cell has to satisfy both at once, which is usually the most useful place to start.

Modes

  • Campaign — 200 handcrafted puzzles in 12 chapters, in a deliberate teaching order.
  • Today's puzzle — one daily puzzle, the same for everyone, with an optional streak.
  • Zen — endless generated puzzles at a level you pick, no timer pressure and no fail state.
  • Challenge — a run that climbs in difficulty; wrong answers cost hearts.
  • Gallery & Creator — build your own puzzles, keep them in collections, and share them.

Stuck on a puzzle

The hamburger menu in the top right has hint, which fills in one cell for you, and show incorrect, which marks the cells that are in the wrong place. In Today's puzzle, using "show incorrect" takes that day's run off the leaderboard — the app tells you before you commit.

Community puzzles

Puzzles other players submit appear in the Gallery under Community puzzles. Every submission is reviewed before it's shown to anyone.

Reporting or blocking

Press and hold any community puzzle for three options:

  • Report puzzle — pick a reason. It's hidden for you immediately and sent for review.
  • Block — hides everything from that person, in both the community list and the streak leaderboard.
  • Hide this puzzle — removes just that one, without blocking anyone.

You can undo a block any time under Blocked in Settings. Using VoiceOver or Voice Control? The same three actions are available as accessibility actions on each row, so you don't need the long-press.

Streaks and leaderboards

Your daily streak is kept on your device. Appearing on the public longest-streak board is off unless you turn it on — look for the sharing toggle in daily history. Turning it back off removes your entry from the board. Game Center leaderboards and achievements need you to be signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings.

Accessibility

Settings has a full section for making the board readable: contrast, brightness and saturation ranges, and a color blindness mode that rebuilds puzzles using a perceptual model tuned for the vision you pick, so the steps stay distinguishable rather than just being recolored. There's also Reduce Motion, an adjustable double-tap window, and a magnetism toggle that widens the drop zone around each cell. The app honors the system Reduce Motion setting as well as its own.

Free vs. Full Version

The first four campaign chapters are free, along with one free puzzle in each of Zen and Challenge, and the daily puzzle every day. The Full Version is a one-time purchase that unlocks the rest of the campaign and removes the per-mode limits. If you've bought it before — on this device or another — use restore purchase on the same screen.

Sharing and .kroma files

Puzzles you make can be shared as a link or as a .kroma file. Opening either on a device with Kromatika installed loads the puzzle straight into the app.

Your data

Reset progress, at the bottom of Settings, clears your level and hearts. Deleting the app removes everything it keeps on your device. See the privacy policy for exactly what does and doesn't leave your phone.
